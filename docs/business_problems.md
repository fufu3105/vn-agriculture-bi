# Business Problems

## BP1. Assessing Rice Sourcing Regions — descriptive analytics

Rice procurement and processing enterprises compare potential sourcing areas using provincial production level, long-run growth, and historical annual variability. The output narrows the places where supplier, price, quality, and logistics assessment should begin.

See the [BP1 guide](bp1.md) for implementation and dashboard controls, and the [English report](../deliverables/report/BP1_Rice_Sourcing_Regions.docx) or [Vietnamese report](../deliverables/report/BP1_Danh_gia_vung_thu_mua_lua.docx) for findings and recommendations.

BP2–BP4 are outside the scope of the current BP1 implementation.
